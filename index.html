Presentation

Five-slide logic

  1. Understand: three accounts; cause unresolved.
  2. Assumptions: created, connected, recorded, reached.
  3. Research: one primary test per assumption; records baseline; people/cases explain variation.
  4. Synthesis: analyze each assumption, integrate evidence, connect across service, check interpretation.
  5. Outputs: finding → owner → next test/action → measure.
Close: Discovery is complete when the City can explain whether and where requests break down, how often, under what conditions, and why residents, 311 and Transportation experience the service differently.
